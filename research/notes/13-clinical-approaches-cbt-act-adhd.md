# 13 — Clinical approaches: CBT, ACT/JOMO & adult ADHD

What clinical psychology — not app marketing — uses to treat problematic use and
distractibility. These give the product an evidence-based *content* layer to pair with the
*environmental* layer (friction, defaults, JITAI).

## CBT — the most-studied, best-evidenced treatment for problematic use
Cognitive Behavioral Therapy is the **most frequently studied and supported** intervention
for problematic internet/smartphone/gaming/social-media use.
- Meta-analysis of psychological treatments for problematic use of internet/games/social
  media/IM: significant reductions favoring treatment, **pooled SMD ≈ −1.53** (large). (MDPI,
  *IJERPH* 2025 — citation-only; PDF JS-gated.)
- Manualized programs (e.g., **STICA**) use three phases: **psychoeducation + motivation →
  behavioral analysis + exposure → relapse prevention / stabilization.**

**Transferable CBT techniques for the product (lightweight, in-app):**
- **Behavioral analysis / self-monitoring:** identify the cue → routine → reward of *your*
  compulsive use. (Our analytics already surface when/where capture happens — note 10.)
- **Cognitive restructuring:** challenge the automatic thought ("I'll just check quickly").
- **Stimulus control:** change the environment to remove cues (= trigger-stripping, defaults).
- **Relapse prevention & lapse-forgiveness:** plan for slips without shame (matches Lally,
  note 07 — a slip ≠ failure).
- **Implementation intentions** (note 06) are essentially CBT's if-then coping plans.

## ACT & JOMO — the values-based alternative to restriction
Restriction fights the urge; **Acceptance & Commitment Therapy (ACT)** reroutes it toward
what the person actually values.
- ACT core: **accept** the urge/discomfort without acting on it, defuse from the thought, and
  take **values-aligned action.** Reframes **FOMO → JOMO** (Joy of Missing Out).
- Evidence: higher **JOMO** is negatively associated with social-media addiction, mediated by
  **lower loneliness and psychological distress** (SEM study, 2025). JOMO correlates with
  mindfulness and self-compassion.
- Predictors of problematic binge/scroll include **escape/mood-regulation motives** (note 11)
  — precisely what ACT targets, where pure blocking does not.

**Why this matters strategically:** restriction-only tools trigger reactance and get
uninstalled (note 06). A **values-based layer** ("what do you actually want your evening
for?") gives the freed attention a *destination* (substitution, note 07) and supports the
**autonomy + competence** needs (SDT, note 06) that make change durable. It's the humane,
pull-toward-values complement to the push-away-from-triggers friction.

## Adult ADHD — digital interventions (the user's target population)
Most ADHD digital-intervention research is on children; **adult** evidence is thinner but
growing and relevant here:
- Guidelines: adult ADHD → **multimodal** (meds + psychosocial); strong patient demand for
  **non-pharmacological** options; digital tools offer scalable, low-threshold access.
- **Digital CBT apps** (e.g., *attexis* RCT — citation-only, PDF JS-gated) show promise for
  reducing **inattention and impulsivity** and improving executive function — **but effects
  are strongest with high adherence, and overall evidence quality is low.** (Adherence caveat
  loops straight back to the durability problem, note 12.)
- **Exercise** (note 09) has some of the better ADHD evidence; combined cognitive-physical
  programs (e.g., START) are being trialed.
- **Mindfulness-based interventions for adult ADHD**: systematic review support for symptom
  improvement (note 09 mechanism: trains executive attention).

**Framing caveat (note 03):** serve the *behavior*, not a diagnosis. Use ADHD evidence to
inform mechanisms, but don't gate the product behind a clinical label — the same tools help
the diagnosed and the "digitally distractible."

## Product implications
1. **Two-layer design:** *environmental* (friction, defaults, JITAI — the durable base) +
   *content/skills* (CBT self-monitoring, if-then plans, ACT values prompts — the meaning).
2. **Borrow CBT's proven moves** as light in-app features (self-monitoring from existing
   analytics; cognitive-restructuring/if-then prompts at the friction moment).
3. **Add a values/JOMO layer**, not just blocking — give freed attention a destination and
   preserve autonomy.
4. **Lean on exercise + mindfulness** as the evidence-backed *substitute* actions to offer.
5. **Don't over-promise clinical/EF gains** (evidence is low-quality; brain-training doesn't
   transfer — note 09). Position as behavior/environment support, not therapy or a cure.
