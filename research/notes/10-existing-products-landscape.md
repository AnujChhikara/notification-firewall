# 10 — Existing products landscape (so we don't just clone one)

The user's directive: build from *how humans work*, **not** "any random app that told you it
would help." So we survey the field to (a) learn what's validated, (b) find the gap.

## The main players
| App | Core mechanism | Evidence / durability | Weakness vs the science |
|---|---|---|---|
| **One Sec** | Friction + breath + "continue?" prompt before target app (self-nudge) | **Best-evidenced**: PNAS RCT, 36% abandon, ~37% fewer opens over 6 wks, didn't fully fade | Generic; not personalized to context/profile; no restorative substitute offered |
| **Opal** | Scheduled blocking + "Focus Score" coaching/insights | Reviewers: more durable than gamification; awareness+coaching helps | Blocking → reactance risk; still willpower-adjacent |
| **Forest** | Gamified reward (grow a tree; it dies if you leave) | **Fades fastest** once novelty dies (~weeks) | Extrinsic gamification; loss-aversion streak = anti-Lally, breeds guilt |
| **Freedom** | Hard cross-device blocklists/schedules | Good for strict blocking | Pure suppression; no substitution; autonomy-undermining |
| **Apple Screen Time / Digital Wellbeing** | Awareness dashboards + limits (easily bypassed) | Weak; limits are dismissible | Awareness ≠ behavior change; no friction at the moment |

## What the field has collectively learned
1. **Friction at the moment of temptation is the winning primitive** (One Sec's RCT is the
   strongest evidence any of these has).
2. **Awareness alone is weak** — dashboards don't change behavior (Apple/Digital Wellbeing).
3. **Gamified streaks fade fastest** and conflict with lapse-forgiveness (note 07).
4. **Hard blocking triggers reactance** and gets uninstalled (note 06).
5. **Everyone hits the ~3-week cliff.** Nobody has convincingly solved *durability.*
6. **The winning combo** (per reviewers): **awareness + friction + replacement** — but no
   single app does all three well, grounded in mechanism.

## The gap = our opportunity
No mainstream app is built as a **coherent counter-design to the attention economy grounded
in cognitive science**, that:
- Combines **friction/self-nudge** (One Sec's proven primitive) with **context/profile
  awareness** (which the *existing Notification Firewall already has* — profiles, rules,
  DND control, an on-device pipeline),
- Offers a **restorative substitute** at the moment of temptation (not just a block),
- **Preserves autonomy** (nudge + override, not jail),
- **Forgives lapses** and explicitly designs for the **60–250-day** horizon to beat the
  3-week cliff,
- Externalizes the **internal trigger** ("you tend to open this when bored at 11pm"),
- and refuses the fake stuff (no brain-training claims, no "dopamine detox" branding).

## Strategic fit with what we've already built
Notification Firewall's existing bones map onto the science unusually well:
- **Profiles + rules + conditions** → an engine for **implementation intentions** (if-then).
- **Notification pipeline + bucket executor** → environmental **trigger-stripping** and
  friction insertion.
- **DND control** → protecting **unbroken blocks / flow** (note 05) and **sleep** (note 09).
- **Analytics** → **externalizing internal triggers** (when/where you get captured).

The rebrand isn't a rewrite — it's **repointing the existing machine from "smarter DND" to
"a science-based counter-design to attention capture,"** and adding the two proven pieces the
field underuses: **moment-of-temptation friction with autonomy**, and **restorative
substitution**, engineered to survive past week 3.
